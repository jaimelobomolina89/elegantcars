# Brand logo sources

These logos were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and converted to PNG. Commons lists each one as **public domain** (too simple for copyright protection), but they are still **registered trademarks** of their owners. Using them to identify the brands of cars you sell is generally fine. They must not suggest that elegantcars is endorsed by, or an official partner of, any of these brands unless that's true. Check each brand's guidelines, or use the files from its official press kit, before going live.

The brands' emblems (Ferrari's prancing horse, Lamborghini's bull, the Porsche crest and the Aston Martin wings) are not freely licensed on Commons. To use them, get the files from each brand's official media site and save them here under the same names.
